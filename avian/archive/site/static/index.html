Listening comparison

Hear more labelled calls

Best evidence groups independent review outcomes first, then uses review score, BirdNET score, audio contrast and recency as separate tie-breakers.

These are other archived BirdNET claims carrying the exact same scientific species label. They are recorded call segments—not unique birds or proof that every identification is correct. Audio contrast is broadband P95–P20 frame-level variation, not SNR: a gust, vehicle or another bird can raise it, while a clean sustained song can score low. More-negative quiet-frame dBFS means a lower-energy portion of the clip.

Methods & custody

About this record

A private, tailnet-only listening archive designed to be locally owned and auditable.

What is kept

Accepted BirdNET recognition claims, timestamps, raw scores, configuration context and content-hashed clips. Reviews annotate claims; they do not rewrite them.

What counts are not

Recognition counts are vocalisation segments—not unique birds, population estimates, coordinates, or calibrated probabilities of truth.

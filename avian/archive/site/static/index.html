How the listening changed

Model, range and location-policy changes are recorded as epochs so a technical change cannot masquerade as an ecological one.

    What is kept

    All BirdNET detections accepted at the configured threshold, their timestamps, raw scores, model settings and content-hashed MP3 clips. Later Perch, Bayesian or human judgments live in separate review fields.

    What the number is not

    Detection counts are recognised vocalisation segments. They are not unique birds, population estimates, or calibrated probabilities of truth.
